## Decisions

Significant decisions are recorded in [`{{path}}/`]({{path}}/README.md) as `{{id_prefix}}-####`.

**Check the admission rule before writing one** — all five criteria must hold, or the content
belongs in the document that already owns the topic. That rule is what keeps the directory from
becoming a second, contradicting documentation tree.

Records are **immutable**: supersede, never edit, and never delete — including rejected ones. The
reason something was not done is the answer to a question that will be asked again.
