## Findings

**Noticed something out of scope?** Record it in
[`{{path}}`]({{path}}) via **`/record-finding`** — severity, priority, evidence, when to act, how
to fix — instead of dropping it in prose or fixing it inside an unrelated change.

A finding is the observation; a work item is the decision. Findings are **rows**, items are
**files**: recording one is meant to cost almost nothing.

A finding is closed as **promoted**, **fixed** or **dismissed**, always with a written reason.
A dismissal without one is the same observation being recorded again next month.
